For an internal team document, the goal is not to create a polished marketing piece—it’s to ensure that everyone understands exactly what AreaCodes is, where it’s going, and what success looks like.

I would structure the Business Overview into the following sections:

1. Executive Summary (1 page)

A high-level overview that anyone can read in 2 minutes.

Include:

* What AreaCodes is.
* The problem it solves.
* Who it serves.
* How it makes money.
* Current stage of the business.
* Long-term vision.

Example:

AreaCodes is a local discovery and voucher marketplace connecting consumers with independent businesses through exclusive offers and discounts. Businesses pay a subscription fee to promote offers, while consumers access the platform free of charge. The business is currently launching in Brighton as a pilot market with plans to expand nationally following validation of product-market fit.

⸻

2. Mission, Vision & Values

Mission

Why AreaCodes exists.

Example:

To empower independent local businesses by helping them attract and retain loyal customers.

Vision

What success looks like in 5–10 years.

Example:

To become the UK’s leading platform for discovering and supporting independent businesses.

Core Values

Examples:

* Support Local
* Community First
* Authenticity

3. The Problem

Explain the market pain points.

Consumer Problems

* Rising cost of living.
* Difficulty discovering quality independent businesses.
* Existing discount platforms often focus on chains.

Business Problems

* High customer acquisition costs.
* Limited marketing budgets.
* Difficulty competing with national chains.
* Lack of measurable, affordable local marketing channels.
