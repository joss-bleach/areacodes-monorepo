01 Executive Summary

AreaCodes connects consumers with independent local businesses through exclusive offers. Businesses pay a monthly subscription. Consumers use it free. Brighton is the pilot. National expansion follows once it works.

02 Mission, Vision & Values
Mission

Empower independent local businesses to attract and keep loyal customers, without selling out.

Vision

The UK's leading platform for discovering and supporting independent businesses.

Core Values
  • Local over corporate
  • Culture over convenience
  • Truth over polish
  • Community over competition
  • Accessible. Never cheap.
03 The Problem
For Consumers
  • Cost of living is rising
  • Good independents are hard to find
  • Discount platforms default to chains
For Businesses
  • Customer acquisition is expensive
  • Marketing budgets are thin
  • National chains dominate reach and spend
  • Affordable, measurable local marketing doesn't exist
04 Our Solution
For Consumers
  • Discover local independents
  • Save money on everyday spend
  • Access exclusive offers
  • Find businesses they'd otherwise miss
For Businesses
  • Affordable promotion with no setup cost
  • More footfall, more direct sales
  • Acquire customers on a small budget
  • Redemption tracking and analytics dashboard
05 Business Model
Revenue Streams
  • Monthly subscription fees from businesses (primary)
  • Premium business plans
  • Featured listings and sponsored placements
  • Event partnerships
  • White-label city partnerships
Business Customers
  • Independent restaurants and cafés
  • Bars and pubs
  • Retail stores
  • Beauty and wellness providers
  • Fitness businesses
  • Local service businesses
Consumer Customers
  • Students and young professionals
  • Local residents and families
  • Visitors and tourists
06 Product Overview
Consumer Features
  • Browse and search by category and location
  • Redeem vouchers in-venue
  • Save favourites
  • User accounts and notifications
Business Features
  • Business profile management
  • Offer creation and management
  • Redemption tracking
  • Analytics dashboard
Technology

Web platform and mobile application. Current status: MVP.

07 Target Market
Initial Market

Brighton. Dense independent scene, large student population, tourism, walkable, strong community culture.

Expansion Targets
  • Bristol
  • Manchester
  • Leeds
  • Edinburgh
08 Competitive Landscape
Existing Players
  • Groupon
  • UNiDAYS
  • Student Beans
  • Local loyalty schemes
  • Facebook community groups
  • City guides and print directories
Why AreaCodes Wins
  • Local-first. Built for independent businesses, not chains.
  • Independent-only. Consumers trust that.
  • Subscription pricing independents can afford
  • City-by-city, so each market gets done properly
  • Brand people want in their pocket
09 Go-To-Market Strategy

Consumer Acquisition
