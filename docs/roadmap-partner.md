# Areacodes — Where We Are & What's Next

Areacodes is a voucher platform driving sales to local, independent businesses and giving customers great deals whilst supporting local.

---

## Where We Are

The core platform is built. Businesses can register, create their profile, and publish vouchers. Customers can browse the map and discover offers. The admin tools are in place to manage the platform.

What we don't have yet is users — we're pre-launch, which is the right moment to complete the remaining pieces before opening the doors.

---

## What's Coming

### 1. Customer Accounts
Customers will be able to sign up and log in — on both the web app and the mobile app — using email or social login (Google, Apple). This is the foundation everything else builds on: without an account, a customer can browse but can't save or use a voucher.

**What this means:** customers can start building a relationship with the platform — saving offers they like and coming back to use them.

### 2. The Full Voucher Flow
Once accounts are in place, the complete journey becomes live:

- A customer **saves** a voucher they like
- When they're in-store, they tap **"Use Now"** — this generates a unique code valid for 2 hours
- They present the code at the till

The 2-hour window and unique-per-customer codes mean offers can't be screenshotted and shared outside the app — protecting the integrity of each business's promotion.

**What this means:** businesses publish a voucher, customers use it in-store. The core product loop is live.

### 3. POS Integrations
For businesses using Square or Zettle (the dominant till systems amongst independents in Brighton), Areacodes will connect directly to their point-of-sale. This means redemptions are verified automatically — the platform can confirm a voucher was genuinely used, not just generated.

Businesses get a dashboard showing exactly how many vouchers were redeemed, giving them real data on what their offers are driving.

**What this means:** businesses can see their return from Areacodes in black and white. This is the core value proposition for onboarding.

### 4. Mobile App
A native iOS and Android app for customers, feature-equivalent to the web experience. Customers can browse, save, and redeem vouchers from their phone — and opt in to push notifications when a business they follow publishes a new offer.

**What this means:** Areacodes is in customers' pockets. Businesses get a direct line to notify their followers of new deals.

---

### 5. Payments & Subscriptions

Businesses pay a flat monthly subscription (£20/month at launch, configurable) to list on the platform. A free pilot is offered to onboard early businesses — duration is configurable. Card details are captured at the end of registration via Stripe; no charge is made until the pilot ends, at which point billing starts automatically.

Card capture is the only gate to publishing vouchers — no manual approval step. This keeps onboarding frictionless while filtering out frivolous signups.

Businesses manage their own billing (card updates, invoices, cancellation) via the Stripe Customer Portal. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. If a payment fails, a 7-day grace period applies before vouchers are suspended.

Future revenue layers (sponsored listings, promoted placements) are planned but out of scope for launch.

---

## The Order of Play

These pieces build on each other in sequence:

**Accounts → Voucher flow → POS integrations → Payments → Mobile app**

Each phase is independently releasable — we don't need to wait for POS integrations to start onboarding customers, and the mobile app can follow the web launch. The goal is to get businesses and customers using the core loop as soon as possible, then layer in verification, payments, and mobile on top.
